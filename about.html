About Urban Thread

Urban Thread is a fictional local streetwear business created for this project. The store offers stylish and budget-friendly clothing such as T-shirts, hoodies, jeans, caps, and sneakers.

Urban Thread is aimed at customers who enjoy modern street style and need comfortable clothing for everyday wear.

Our Mission

Our mission is to offer stylish clothes at affordable prices while giving customers a friendly shopping experience.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a well-known local streetwear brand that connects with young members of the community.

Our Target Audience

Urban Thread mainly targets teenagers, college students, and young adults between the ages of 16 and 30 who are interested in modern streetwear.
