1. Introduction to the Project

Urban Thread is a fictional streetwear business created as part of the WEDE5020 Portfolio of Evidence (POE). The website serves as an online platform where visitors can learn about the business, browse its clothing range, view prices, and communicate with the business through enquiry and contact forms.

The concept is based on providing modern and affordable streetwear for young people. The intended customers are teenagers, college students, and young adults aged between 16 and 30.

Development took place across three stages. The first stage involved planning the website and building its basic HTML structure. The second stage concentrated on the visual design and making the website suitable for different devices. The third stage involves adding JavaScript interactions, improving form handling, applying SEO techniques, testing the website, and preparing the final project for submission.

2. Project Aims

The purpose of the Urban Thread website is to establish a basic online presence for the fictional clothing brand and make its information accessible to potential customers.

The main objectives are to:

* Introduce the Urban Thread brand and its purpose.
* Present clothing items with their descriptions and prices.
* Give visitors background information about the business.
* Provide a way for customers to enquire about products.
* Make business contact details available to visitors.
* Develop a website that is straightforward to navigate.
* Ensure that the layout can adapt to desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
* Maintain consistent navigation throughout the website.
* Use JavaScript to provide additional interactive functionality.
* Apply suitable SEO practices to organise website content.
* Improve usability through form validation and interactive components.
